# Image sources

NASA planetary images, downloaded 2026-10-25. Images are bundled locally, so the app works offline.

Mercury
Source: https://science.nasa.gov/solar-system/planets/
Image: https://science.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/pia15162-mercury-basins-messenger-16x9-1.jpg?w=1024
Description: Mercury is gray with bright white patches, and craters visible in this image from the MESSENGER spacecraft.

Venus
Source: https://science.nasa.gov/solar-system/planets/
Image: https://science.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/venus-mariner-10-pia23791-fig2-16x9-1.jpg?w=1024
Description: A serene-looking Venus with creamy white, and tan clouds.

Earth
Source: https://science.nasa.gov/solar-system/planets/
Image: https://science.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/blue-marble-apollo-17-16x9-1.jpg?w=1024
Description: A view of Earth from Apollo 17 showing the blue ocean, reddish brown landmasses, and wispy, white clouds.

Mars
Source: https://science.nasa.gov/solar-system/planets/
Image: https://science.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/mars-full-globe-16x9-1.jpg?w=1024
Description: Mars is a reddish brown in this image from a spacecraft. A deep gash is visible across the center of the planet.

Jupiter
Source: https://science.nasa.gov/solar-system/planets/
Image: https://science.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/jupiter-marble-pia22946-16x9-1.jpg?w=1024
Description: A view of Jupiter's Great Red Spot and colorful cloud bands of tan, brown, white, and orange as seen from the Juno spacecraft.

NASA image use guidance: https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/

Jupiter image (PIA22946): enhanced image by Kevin M. Gill (CC-BY), using NASA/JPL-Caltech/SwRI/MSSS data. The bundled copy is resized/cropped by NASA for its planets overview. Original image page: https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/images/pia22946-jupiter-marble/

Saturn, Uranus, and Neptune images: https://science.nasa.gov/solar-system/planets/
Earth detail and Jupiter detail are cropped versions of the corresponding bundled images.
